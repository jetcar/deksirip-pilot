# Author Profile

**Vladimir Kruglov is an independent researcher.**

He works on DEKSIRIP, a research model of decentralised public economics and a
possible bounded experimental test of its institutions.

Main topics:

- institutions for allocating common resources;
- social dividend and basic provision;
- transparent coordination of needs, resources and projects;
- independent audit and resident participation;
- limits of digital tools and AI in economic governance.

DEKSIRIP is not a finished political programme. The project seeks academic
criticism, relevant precedents and a determination of which elements could be
tested in a small voluntary pilot.
