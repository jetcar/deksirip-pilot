# Concept Note: A Local Pilot for Transparent Common-Resource Allocation

**Version:** 0.1 draft  
**Purpose:** academic review and possible research collaboration  
**Scope:** voluntary local pilot; not a proposal for immediate national reform

**To run something now:** the [pilot kit](pilot-kit-en.md) is executable by a
cooperative, energy community, dormitory or small municipality without a host
institution and without the author. This note describes the larger pilot that
does need one.

## 1. Summary

This concept note proposes a small, voluntary and reversible pilot combining
several mechanisms that have been tested separately in China and elsewhere:
common or public ownership of selected productive assets, unconditional basic
services, a resident social dividend, transparent cost information, voluntary
project funding, strategic reserves and independent audit.

The proposal does not claim that these mechanisms are new individually. The
research question is whether their combination can improve access to basic
goods, productive investment and resilience while keeping decision-making
visible and limiting rent extraction.

## 2. Research question

Can a bounded community coordinate production and allocation without either a
central authority assigning every output target or private owners receiving a
permanent claim on common productive assets?

## 3. Proposed institutional package

1. **Basic provision.** A defined basket of essential food, housing, healthcare
   and education is provided as a right of residence, subject to transparent
   capacity limits during the pilot.
2. **Social dividend.** Residents receive an equal periodic share of the
   pilot's distributable common product. The dividend does not confer ownership
   of land, factories or infrastructure.
3. **Cost information.** Published prices show physical cost, capacity,
   scarcity and relevant external damage rather than private profit.
4. **Project funding.** Residents may direct part of their discretionary share
   to proposals. Funding is released in stages and can be stopped when evidence
   changes.
5. **Reserves.** The community sets reserve targets for food, medicine, energy
   and other critical inputs before a crisis occurs.
6. **Governance.** Executives have limited mandates, are replaceable, and are
   subject to parallel audit and public reporting.

## 4. Pilot boundary

The pilot should begin with one locality, cooperative network or specially
authorized development area. It should have:

- a defined resident population;
- a separable budget and asset register;
- legal authority for the selected services and assets;
- a conventional external economy for trade and comparison;
- an independent evaluation team;
- an accessible exit route for residents and participating organizations.

The pilot should not attempt to replace the national economy. Its purpose is
to test a limited institutional interface and identify failure modes early.

## 5. Evaluation

The study should pre-register outcomes before implementation. Candidate
measures include access to essential goods, household security, working time,
productive output, project completion, reserve reliability, inequality,
resident participation, trust, administrative cost and ecological impact.

Results should be compared with matched localities or with the locality's own
pre-pilot baseline. A negative result is useful if it identifies which
mechanism failed and under what conditions.

## 6. What this proposal is not

- not a request to adopt a complete ideology;
- not a claim that China has no relevant experience;
- not a plan to remove all markets or professional administration;
- not a promise that transparency eliminates political conflict;
- not a request for implementation before legal, ethical and economic review.

## 7. Requested collaboration

Researchers are invited to challenge the model, identify Chinese precedents,
redesign the pilot boundary, specify legal constraints, propose control groups
and estimate whether the package is testable at all.

The desired first outcome is a research critique, not an endorsement.

## 8. Scope of this public note

The model's high-level logic is set out in the [model
framework](model-framework-en.md). Technical architecture and prototype
specifications are intentionally kept outside this public first-stage package.
They can be reviewed separately after the conceptual proposal has received
initial academic criticism.
