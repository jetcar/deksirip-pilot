# Evidence Matrix: Chinese Precedents and Remaining Gaps

This is a preliminary map, not a systematic literature review.

| DEKSIRIP element | Chinese precedent | What it demonstrates | What remains untested |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot-first reform | Special economic zones and pilot free-trade zones | Rules can be tested locally and replicated | Whether the same method can test a full allocation package |
| Basic services and reduced inequality | Zhejiang Common Prosperity Demonstration Zone | Public services and income-gap reduction can be coordinated at provincial scale | Whether basic provision can be separated from employment and private ownership |
| Collective assets and dividends | Rural collective economy and village dividend projects | Collective assets can generate local income and dividends | Equal resident-wide dividend and protection against insider control |
| Public-resource ownership | Natural-resource asset ownership and revenue-sharing pilots | Public assets can be registered, managed and linked to revenue distribution | Transparent citizen control over the full asset portfolio |
| Strategic reserves | National and local food, energy and medical reserve systems | Advance provisioning is institutionally possible | Combining reserve decisions with open resident participation |
| Cost and scarcity information | Pilot market reforms and free-trade-zone institutional experiments | Prices and rules can be used as controlled signals | Cost pricing without private profit as the main coordinating incentive |
| Staged project funding | Public investment and local collective projects | Projects can be financed through administrative and collective channels | Voluntary resident allocation with automatic review and withdrawal |

## Preliminary conclusion

The strongest Chinese precedent is not a single finished model. It is a
culture of bounded institutional experimentation. The proposed contribution is
to combine existing mechanisms into one explicitly evaluated local package.
