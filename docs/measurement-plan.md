# Measurement Plan

## Primary outcomes

1. **Basic access:** share of households receiving the defined basket on time;
   shortages, waiting time and unmet need.
2. **Security:** income volatility, food insecurity, housing loss and emergency
   debt.
3. **Productive capacity:** output per working hour, equipment use, new skills
   and project completion.
4. **Allocation quality:** abandoned projects, revision time, reserve
   reliability and mismatch between forecasts and actual demand.
5. **Distribution:** income and wealth concentration, dividend coverage and
   access by vulnerable groups.
6. **Governance:** audit findings, complaints resolved, replacement events,
   participation and perceived trust.
7. **External cost:** energy use, emissions, ecological damage and resources
   imported from outside the pilot.

## Evaluation rules

- publish definitions before data collection;
- separate outcomes from interpretation;
- report missing data and failed indicators;
- preserve a pre-pilot baseline;
- use matched comparison areas where possible;
- publish negative and inconclusive results;
- do not aggregate a successful subproject into a claim about the whole model.

## Stop conditions

The pilot must pause for independent review if it produces persistent basic-
goods shortages, material falsification of accounts, coercive participation,
unreviewable concentration of authority, or serious harm to non-participants.
