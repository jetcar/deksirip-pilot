# Outreach Plan for Chinese Researchers

## Goal

Obtain critical academic review of the model and learn which elements could be
studied in a Chinese institutional context. The first message should not ask for
implementation, funding or political endorsement.

## Sequence

1. Fill in the author's name, affiliation or independent-researcher status, and
   working email address.
2. Select three recipients for the first wave based on research fit.
3. Add one personalised sentence for each recipient based on their work or
   institute.
4. Send a short Chinese message with the Chinese PDF and repository link.
5. Send one polite follow-up after 7–10 business days.
6. Do not mass-mail, use BCC, or imply readiness for immediate implementation.
7. Record replies, criticism and suggested sources in a contact log.

## Priority areas

### First wave

- **Income distribution and common prosperity:** Zhejiang University Institute
  for Common Prosperity and Development.
- **Public finance and institutional design:** Department of Economics, Tsinghua
  University School of Economics and Management.
- **Development, public finance and reform:** National School of Development,
  Peking University.

### Second wave

- **Labour, development and shared provision:** School of Economics, Renmin
  University.
- **Income, social policy and evaluation:** Renmin University Common Prosperity
  Institute and related research centres.

## Message package

- subject: “请求对地方经济试点提案进行学术评议”;
- 120–180 Chinese words;
- one paragraph describing the model;
- one paragraph explaining the recipient-specific fit;
- three concrete questions for criticism;
- Chinese concept-note PDF;
- public repository link;
- author's name and contact.

## Send-readiness gate

The message should be sent only after these fields are filled:

```text
Author name:
Affiliation or independent-researcher status:
Email:
Acceptable reply languages:
```

Until these fields are filled, the materials are ready for review but not for
sending on behalf of an unidentified author.
