# Short Invitation to a Researcher

**Subject:** Request for critical review of a local economic pilot proposal

Dear colleague,

I have prepared a proposal for a small, voluntary and reversible economic pilot.
It combines basic provision, a share of the distributable result of common
assets, transparent resource and cost information, staged project support,
strategic reserves and independent audit.

The proposal does not claim to introduce a new economic ideology or a proven
system. Its narrower practical question is whether a bounded combination of
institutions found separately in China and elsewhere can be tested while
preserving voluntary participation, a right of exit and comparable evaluation.

I would particularly value criticism in four areas:

1. the economic hypothesis;
2. Chinese and international precedents;
3. legal and institutional limits on a pilot;
4. the suitability of the indicators and comparison group.

A negative conclusion would be useful. If the idea is infeasible or combines
incompatible mechanisms, that criticism would help narrow the question or reject
the design.

The materials are available in Russian, English and Chinese. The technical tools
— a resource register, electronic voting and an AI assistant — are placed in
appendices and are not the starting purpose of the project.

Sincerely,

Vladimir Kruglov  
Independent researcher, DEKSIRIP project
