# Official sources for the comparative section

This is a short source register for the public concept note. It records
institutional precedents without claiming that any one of them implements the
full DEKSIRIP model.

## China: bounded institutional experimentation

- [Zhejiang Common Prosperity Demonstration Zone plan](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fggz/qykf/xxjc/202107/t20210728_1291943.html) — provincial framework for coordinated common-prosperity experiments.
- [Zhejiang's first batch of replicable practices](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xxgk/zcfb/tz/202303/t20230323_1351675.html) — official examples of practices selected for replication.
- [Pinghu collective-development pilots](https://www.mof.gov.cn/zhengwuxinxi/xinwenlianbo/zhejiangcaizhengxinxilianbo/202401/t20240124_3927140.htm) — local collective-economy and common-development experiments.
- [Natural-resource asset ownership pilots](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/201904/14/438034/article.html) — institutional treatment of public natural-resource assets.
- [Natural-resource asset management guideline](https://english.www.gov.cn/policies/latestreleases/202607/13/content_WS6a54ec08c6d00ca5f9a0c2b4.html) — official framework for natural-resource asset management.
- [China's pilot free-trade zones](https://english.www.gov.cn/services/investment/202602/04/content_WS69829975c6d00ca5f9a08e8a.html) — national description of pilot zones as bounded policy experiments.

## How to read these sources

They support the narrower claim that China uses geographically bounded pilots,
collective-asset institutions and public-resource management mechanisms. They
do not establish that China has already tested the complete DEKSIRIP package of
basic provision, resident allocation, common-asset dividends, transparent
resource information and independent audit in one integrated system.
